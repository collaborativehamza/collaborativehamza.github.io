About

Five years of figuring out why people stay.

I'm MD Aamir Hamza, a video editor based in India, specialised in short-form video built to actually hold attention, not just fill a feed.

I started making content in 2021, self-taught, no equipment, no plan. Every project since has been about figuring out the mechanics behind why a viewer stays or scrolls, not just how to edit a clip.

I take on: research the topic properly, understand what that specific audience actually responds to, write a script structured to retain them, then edit and pace it for the platform it's going out on.
